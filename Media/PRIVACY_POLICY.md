This Privacy Policy explains how **Ultimate Puzzles Dinos** (the “App”) accesses, uses, stores and shares information. The App is a dinosaur puzzle game intended for children and families and is distributed in **Free** and **Premium** editions.

---

## 1. Data stored by the game

Ultimate Puzzles Dinos does not require users to create an account.

The App stores game data locally on the user's device, including:

- unlocked levels;
- best star scores;
- game progress;
- audio settings;
- vibration settings;
- other gameplay preferences.

This information is used only to provide the game's functionality. VRM Games does not operate an online player-profile database for this game and does not receive this local progress data.

Local game data remains on the device until the user resets the App data, clears the App storage, or uninstalls the App.
